HOK Team Playbook

How we run client work in HOK  ·  For Nida, Arbia, Kamala, Imtiyaz and everyone who joins  ·  Nov 2026

The whole idea in one line: an event is what the client sees; a task is one person's job; a template is the list of steps both follow.

If you remember only this page, you can use HOK.

1. Seven words to know

WordWhat it meansExample (Zyra)
ClientA business we work for. Only its team (and Nida) can see it.Zyra, team: Arbia
EventClient work with a date. The client sees it in their portal and watches its stage move.“Zyra reels – week 46”
TaskOne job for one person, with a due date. Internal: the client never sees it.“Edit reel 1” for Imtiyaz
TemplateThe steps (stages) an event or task goes through. Only Nida makes them.“Starter: Edit one reel”
StageOne step in a template. Work moves stage by stage.Rough cut → Final cut
ApprovalA stage someone must say “yes” to before work can move on.Arbia approves the rough cut
PartA smaller event or task made from a stage. The stage waits until all its parts are finished.5 reels = 5 parts of “Editing”

Also: My to-dos are your private notes with reminders. Nobody else sees them, not even Nida.

2. Three golden rules

✓ Do

  • One template per kind of work, e.g. “Edit one reel”. Need 5 reels? Use Add part → How many: 5.
  • Give an event its real day (shoot day, posting day). Put deadlines on tasks.
  • Write the brief and add links (raw footage, references) when you create the task.

✗ Don't

  • Don't make stages called “Reel 1, Reel 2 … Reel 5”. That's what parts are for.
  • Don't stretch one event over a whole week to mean “this week's work”. Make parts with due dates instead.
  • Don't send files on WhatsApp. Paste the Drive link in the task's Delivered work.

3. A real week, step by step: “Zyra reels – week 46”

#WhoWhat they do in HOK
1NidaOnce only: makes the templates in Templates. The event template is “Starter: Weekly reels (client)”: Planning and scripting → Shooting → Editing → Client review (the client approves) → Scheduled. The task template is “Starter: Edit one reel”: Brief received → Rough cut → SMM review (Arbia or Nida approves) → Final cut (Nida approves) → Delivered.
2ArbiaOpens Zyra → New event, picks the weekly reels template, and sets the shoot day as the date.
3ArbiaMoves the event through Planning and Shooting with Move stage. Zyra sees each step in their portal.
4ArbiaAt the Editing stage: Add part → Task → template “Edit one reel” → assignee Imtiyaz → How many: 5, with a brief, raw footage link and due date. HOK makes “Edit reel 1 … 5”. The event's Editing stage now waits for 5 parts.
5ImtiyazSees the 5 tasks on his dashboard and calendar. For each one: Brief received → Rough cut, adds the cut under Delivered work, then Request approval.
6ArbiaGets a notification and opens Approvals. Then either Approve, or Request changes with a comment (Imtiyaz moves the reel back, fixes it and asks again).
7NidaApproves each Final cut. Imtiyaz moves the reel to Delivered and Mark complete.
8ArbiaWhen all 5 reels are done, HOK notifies her: “Parts finished”. On each delivered link she clicks Share with client, then moves the event to Client review and Request approval.
9ZyraOpens their portal link and sees “Your approval is needed” with the reels. They click Approve, or Request changes with a note.
10ArbiaAfter Zyra approves, moves the event to Scheduled for posting. Done.

4. When a button is greyed out

The screen says…What it means and what to do
“Needs approval at stage”Someone must approve this stage first. Click Request approval, or ask the approver to check Approvals.
“Waiting for 3 parts at stage”3 parts made from this stage aren't finished yet. Open Parts to see which.
“You can only assign tasks to roles below yours”Managers give tasks to editors, writers, developers and agents. Only Nida assigns to managers.
“View only: you're on this client's team”You can see the task but didn't create it. Ask its creator or Nida.

5. Your daily routine

1Nida (Owner / Admin)

  • Dashboard: overdue tasks, team workload
  • Approvals → To decide: final cuts
  • Keep templates tidy (deactivate old ones)
  • Add people, and set each client's team

2Arbia / Kamala (Managers)

  • Calendar: what's shooting and what's due
  • Approvals → To decide: rough cuts
  • Client comments: reply, mark Solved
  • Create events and give tasks to editors and writers

3Imtiyaz (Editor) and other staff

  • Dashboard → My tasks, oldest due first
  • Read the brief and links before starting
  • Move stages as you go, and add your work under Delivered work
  • Stuck? Mark blocked with a reason

4Everyone

  • My to-dos for personal reminders
  • Turn on push notifications (iPhone: add HOK to the Home Screen)
  • The bell shows what needs you
  • In-app Guide explains each page

The starter templates (“Starter: …”) are examples. Nida can rename or delete them in Templates.
